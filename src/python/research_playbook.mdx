## Overview

This playbook provides research strategies and tool recommendations for different types of claims. The playbook evolves as the agent learns from investigation outcomes.

## Playbook Sections

### Funding Claims

**Suggested Tools:**
- [SEC Filings](https://sec.gov/edgar/search/) - Corporate financial disclosures
- [Corporate Registries](https://www.opencorporates.com/) - Business registration data
- [Bankruptcy Filings](https://www.justice.gov/ust) - Financial distress indicators
- [Tax Authority Filings](https://www.irs.gov/) - Tax liability information

**Default Questioning Strategies:**
1. Ask about the source of funding
2. Verify legitimacy of the entity
3. Check for financial relationships with targets
4. Identify any conflicts of interest

**Default Depth Escalation Rules:**
- Phase 1: Public filings and registries (quick scan)
- Phase 2: Financial statements and tax records (detailed analysis)
- Phase 3: Third-party verification (expert opinion if needed)

**Default Confidence Thresholds:**
- **Low Confidence (< 0.4):** No direct evidence of funding
- **Medium Confidence (0.4 - 0.6):** Inferences from public data
- **High Confidence (> 0.6):** Direct financial records
